## Why M5 and S2 differ

M5 counts core-ready sessions (1,697 of 1,699); the two missing sessions are the quarantined crossover sessions. S2 also excludes sessions that carry a session-level warning (B04, B07, T04, T05, I06), which stay in every KPI. A warning is a diagnostic flag, not a failure, so S2 is lower than M5 without any session being removed. Neither is a measure of accuracy.

## W1 Direct Food Waste Measurement: BLOCKED / SOURCE GAP

The Flavoria system documents lunch-line waste measurement keyed on tray, but the public data available to this project does not contain those records. No direct waste metric is computed. The derived selected meal weight is not waste, and no proxy is estimated from weight, components, duration, weather or anything else.
